## Label

`class` (nominal): `g` gluon, `q` light quark, `t` top quark, `w` W boson, `z` Z boson (label-encoded alphabetically 0–4). The five classes are ~20% each (see `reports/real_data_validation.md`).

## Structure that is NOT used

The Zenodo record (10.5281/zenodo.3602260) also distributes jet images and per-jet particle lists (up to 150 particles). The OpenML copy used here contains only the 16 high-level features, so the structured constituent information is not available to this benchmark. This is a scope limit of the chosen file, not a choice to discard information.
