# Skeptical Reviewer Checklist

Assessed against the repository state at the end of Phase 3. PASS = adequately evidenced for the claims made; PARTIAL = real but bounded gap; FAIL = not met.

| # | Reviewer question | Verdict | Evidence / remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the dataset clearly documented? | **PASS** | `reports/dataset_selection.md` (candidates, license, citation, corrections), `dataset_schema.md` (16 features, statistics), `real_data_validation.{json,md}` (two from-scratch runs byte-identical). The simulated/particle-level nature is stated everywhere; absence of detector simulation is stated as "not described in the source". |
| 2 | Is the baseline reproducible? | **PASS** | FP32 ROC-AUC 0.9343 ± 0.0011 (n = 5); two bit-identical reruns of a frozen configuration; FP32 agrees between CPU and CUDA to ≤ 1e-3. Consistent with (not a reproduction of) the 75% reference in arXiv:2003.06308. |

| 4 | Are PTQ and QAT compared fairly? | **PARTIAL** | A compute-matched FP32 fine-tune control exists (5 seeds) and shows QAT's INT8/INT6 edge is not separable from extra epochs. Remaining asymmetry: QAT recipe is fixed (7 epochs, 0.1× lr, untuned) and PTQ uses naive min/max calibration with no tuning; neither side was hyperparameter-optimized. |
| 5 | Are multiple seeds used? | **PASS (with caveat)** | n = 5 seeds (3 frozen + 2 added), paired analysis, per-seed values shown. n = 5 supports descriptive intervals only; no significance tests are claimed. Checkpoint-level studies use n = 3. |

The 21-run in-experiment latencies are labelled indicative. |
